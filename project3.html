Overview

As part of the SAE Baja intercollegiate engineering competition, our team designed, fabricated, and raced a single-seat off-road vehicle built around a restricted 10hp Briggs & Stratton engine. The competition evaluates vehicles on static design, cost analysis, sales pitch, and dynamic events including hill climb, maneuverability, and a grueling 4-hour endurance race.

My Role & Contributions

Led the powertrain subsystem of the vehicle, designed the gearbox and driveshafts of the vehicle. I designed the CAD, prototyped, performed FEA analysis on the rear driveshaft assembly. I also designed telescopic driveshafts for the first time in the competition to improve the rear suspensions of the vehicle by 15%.

I was also involved in the assembly of the vehicle, manual machining of the components, fixturing and prototyping of the components.
