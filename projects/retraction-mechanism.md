# Electromechanical Retraction Mechanism

## Problem
Design a compact and reliable retraction mechanism capable of controlled motion under mechanical and packaging constraints.

## Constraints
- Limited envelope
- Required repeatability and positioning accuracy
- Load and stiffness requirements
- Manufacturability and assembly constraints

## Key Design Decisions
- Selected mechanism topology based on force and space tradeoffs
- Motor and transmission choice driven by torque-speed requirements
- Bearing and guidance strategy for stiffness and life
- Design for manufacturability considerations

## Analysis & Design
- Hand calculations and CAD-based motion studies
- Tolerance stack-up analysis
- Motor sizing and basic control considerations

## Prototyping & Testing
- Prototype fabrication
- Functional testing and iteration
- Identified failure modes and improvements

## Results
- Achieved smooth and repeatable retraction
- Met packaging and load requirements
- Design validated through testing

## What I’d Improve Next
- Closed-loop motor control
- Weight reduction
- Assembly simplification
