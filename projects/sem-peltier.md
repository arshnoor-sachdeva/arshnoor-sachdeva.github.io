# Water-Cooled Peltier Thermal Control Module for SEM

## Problem
SEM imaging required sub-degree Celsius thermal stability at the sample interface while managing localized heat loads under high-vacuum conditions.

## Constraints
- High vacuum compatibility (materials, sealing)
- Tight spatial envelope
- No airflow
- Vibration and EMI sensitivity
- Condensation risk

## Key Design Decisions
- Selected water-cooled Peltier over passive cooling based on heat flux calculations
- Cold plate material tradeoff (Al vs Cu)
- Vacuum-compatible sealing strategy
- Control bandwidth vs thermal stability tradeoff

## Prototyping & Testing
- Bench validation of thermal performance
- Integration testing in SEM environment

## Results
- Achieved ±0.2°C thermal stability at target interface
- Reduced thermal drift relative to baseline
- Successful integration without imaging artifacts
